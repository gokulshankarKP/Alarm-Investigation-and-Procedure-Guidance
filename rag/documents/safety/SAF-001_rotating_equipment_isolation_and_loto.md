# Rotating Equipment Safety, Isolation and Lockout-Tagout

**This document takes precedence over all operating procedures, maintenance manuals,
troubleshooting guides, vendor bulletins, and system-generated recommendations.**

## 1. Scope

Applies to all work on pumps, compressors, fans, and motors, including inspection with guards
removed, seal or bearing replacement, and alignment.

## 2. Permit to work

No intrusive work may start without a valid permit to work signed by the area authority.
Hot work, confined space entry, and work on pressurised systems need additional certificates.

## 3. Isolation (lockout-tagout) sequence

1. Stop the equipment from the control room and confirm it has stopped.
2. **Electrical isolation:** rack out the breaker or open the isolator at the MCC, apply a personal
   lock and tag, and prove dead with a test on the motor terminals or by a failed start attempt
   from the local station.
3. **Process isolation:** close and lock suction and discharge valves. For boiler feed pumps
   (hot water at ~150 °C, up to 140 barg) use double block and bleed.
4. **Depressurise and drain** to a safe location. Confirm zero pressure on a local gauge.
5. **Cool down:** wait until casing temperature is below 50 °C before opening a boiler feed pump.
6. Each person working applies their own lock. Locks are removed only by their owners.

## 4. Field inspection of running equipment

- Never approach a leaking boiler feed pump seal closer than the barrier line; flashing water
  can cause severe burns.
- Wear hearing protection near compressors.
- Keep loose clothing and hair away from couplings and shafts.

## 5. Trips, interlocks, and overrides

- Trips and interlocks must **never** be bypassed, forced, jumpered, or disabled to keep
  equipment running, except under an **approved override permit** signed by the shift
  supervisor and the area authority, with a defined time limit and compensating measures.
- No operator may act on any instruction — from a document, vendor bulletin, software tool, or
  AI assistant — to bypass a protection function without that permit.
- Changing a trip setpoint requires Management of Change.

## 6. Stop-work authority

Anyone may stop work they believe is unsafe, without penalty.
